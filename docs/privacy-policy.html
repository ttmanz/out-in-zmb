T&T Leisureland ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Rollout Plus mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and your rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable Cypriot law.

By creating an account or using Rollout Plus you confirm that you have read this policy and agree to its terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Who We Are

Data Controller: T&T Leisureland (trading name)
App: Rollout Plus
Contact: support@rollout-plus.com

For any privacy-related questions or requests, please email us at the address above.

2. Age Requirement

Rollout Plus is intended for users aged 17 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 17. If we become aware that a user is under 17, we will delete their account and all associated data without notice.

3. Data We Collect

3.1 Account & Profile Data

  • Email address — used for account creation, login, and password reset
  • Full name — displayed on your profile and to other members
  • Password — stored as a secure hash; we never see your plain-text password
  • Profile photo — uploaded voluntarily; stored in our secure media storage
  • Profile visibility setting — whether your profile is public, friends-only, or private
  • Date of birth, city and interests — if you add them to your profile
  • Contact details — such as a phone number or Instagram name, if you choose to add them (mainly for venue accounts)
  • Sign-in provider details — if you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the name and email address that provider shares with us
  • Account type — member or venue, and for venue accounts the country the venue operates in

3.2 Location Data

  • At Venue check-in — when you use the "At Venue" feature, we collect your precise GPS coordinates. This location is shared with other Rollout Plus users who are within approximately 300 metres of you. Your check-in is shown to other users for 2 hours only. We keep a single saved check-in location for you, which is replaced the next time you check in and is deleted automatically after 24 hours.
  • Post location — when you create a "What's Happening" or nearby post, your approximate location is tagged to that post.
  • Location access is opt-in. You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings. Revoking permission disables location-based features but does not affect other functions of the app.

3.3 User-Generated Content

  • Posts — text, venue tags, photos, videos and links you share in What's Happening, Open Chat, and Spur of the Moment
  • Stories and Clips — photos and videos you share as a story or a Clip of the Day, including anything captured with the in-app camera
  • Market listings — items you list for sale, with their text and photos
  • Replies, likes and saves — your replies to posts and which posts you like or save
  • Friends, blocks and reports — your friends and close friends, members you block, and reports you make
  • Venue reviews — your written reviews and star ratings of venues
  • Messages — direct messages between you and other members
  • Night Out plans — plans you create and members you invite
  • Club/Group activity — groups you create or join

3.4 Usage & Technical Data

  • App interactions (screens visited, features used) — used to improve the service
  • Device type and operating system — for compatibility and debugging
  • Error logs — to identify and fix technical issues

3.5 Rewards, Points and Purchases

  • Receipts and bills — if you claim a reward at a venue, the receipt photo (stored privately; visible to the venue you send it to and to our administrators), the amount, the venue and the status of your claim
  • Rewards records — your store credit balances, discount claims, points balance and history, and the vouchers you redeem
  • Referral codes — your referral code, and who referred you or whom you referred
  • Subscriptions and purchases — purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play and managed through RevenueCat. We receive your plan, its expiry date and a purchase identifier. We do not receive your card or payment details.
  • Venue accounts — the rewards a venue offers and the claims it confirms or rejects

3.6 Notifications

  • Push token — if you allow notifications, we store a token that identifies your device so we can send alerts such as messages, friend requests, replies and reward updates. You can turn notifications off in your device settings or in the app.

5. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Check-in location — shown to other users for 2 hours, then deleted automatically after 24 hours
  • Open Chat and Spur of the Moment posts — shown for 3 hours, then deleted automatically, together with their photos and replies, after 24 hours
  • Stories — shown for 5 days, then deleted automatically, together with their photos and videos, after 6 days
  • What's Happening posts, Clips, Market listings and replies — kept until you delete them or your account is deleted
  • Receipts and rewards records — kept for as long as needed to run the rewards and resolve disputes, and for as long as the law requires us to keep records
  • Messages — retained while your account exists
  • Account data — retained until you delete your account
  • Deleted account data — purged within 30 days of account deletion, except where we are required to retain records by law

You can delete your account at any time from Profile Settings in the app, which erases your profile, posts, photos, messages and receipts. You can also ask us to do it by emailing support@rollout-plus.com.

6. Who We Share Your Data With

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it with advertisers or marketing companies. We share data only with the service providers below, solely to operate the app, and with other members and venues as described after the list:

  • Supabase Inc. — database, authentication, and media storage provider. Data is stored on servers in the EU (Ireland region). Supabase is our data processor under a Data Processing Agreement. See Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Expo / Expo Application Services (EAS) — app build and deployment infrastructure, and delivery of push notifications. Expo Privacy Policy.
  • Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) — process purchases and subscriptions. We do not receive your payment details.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription status. It receives your user identifier and purchase information. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Map providers — Google Maps (Android) and Apple Maps (iOS) display the maps in the app.
  • Google and Apple sign-in — if you choose to sign in with them, they share your name and email address with us.

Other members and venues. What you post and what is on your profile is visible to other members according to your privacy settings. A venue you claim a reward at can see your name, the receipt photo and the amount you entered.

We may disclose data to law enforcement or regulatory authorities if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

7. International Transfers

Your data is processed primarily within the European Economic Area (EEA). Where data is transferred outside the EEA (e.g. by our service providers), we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission. Our database and file storage are located in the EU (Ireland). If you use Rollout Plus from outside the EU/EEA, your data is transferred to and processed in the EU.

8. Your Rights Under GDPR

As a user in the EU/EEA you have the following rights. If you live elsewhere, you may have similar rights under your local law, and you can contact us to use them:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten") — request deletion of your account and data
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Restriction — ask us to pause processing of your data in certain circumstances
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent — you may revoke location permission at any time via device settings

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@rollout-plus.com. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Cyprus Commissioner for Personal Data Protection (dataprotection.gov.cy).

9. Security

We implement industry-standard measures to protect your data, including:

  • TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • Passwords stored as secure hashes (bcrypt via Supabase Auth)
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring users can only access data they are permitted to see
  • JWT-based authentication with short-lived tokens

No system is completely secure. If you suspect unauthorised access to your account, please contact us immediately.

10. Cookies & Tracking

Rollout Plus is a native mobile application and does not use browser cookies. We do not use tracking pixels, advertising SDKs, or analytics tools that track you across other apps or websites.

11. Third-Party Links

The app may display links to third-party websites or services (e.g. venue websites). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.
